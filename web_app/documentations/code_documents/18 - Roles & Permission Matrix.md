Le IMIS is equipped avec pre-defined roles et permissions within le systeme ce govern le extent to which le modules et their functionalities can be accessed. Ce role-based approche ensures visibility of modules, outils et features according to le responsibilities assigned to le role. Le pre-defined roles can be easily updated et modified as per le requirements of le ville via le Settings section of IMIS, et nouveau roles beyond le pre-defined ones can be added as well.
Le Systeme integre d'information municipale (IMIS) est concu pour accueillir plusieurs types d'utilisateurs, chacun jouant un role specifique dans la gestion des services municipaux. Le systeme comprend cinq grandes categories d'utilisateurs :

A.  Municipality: Ce utilisateur type consists of officials working in le  municipality et ward offices. They have varying levels of access based on their department et responsibilities.

B.  Service Provider: Ce utilisateur type inclut private entities ce provide sanitation services within le ville. Ces entities must be registered in le IMS des prestataires de services.

C.  Treatment Plant: Ce utilisateur type represents entities managing le global operation of le traitement plant, which is registered in le IMS des stations de traitement.

D.  Centre d'assistance: Centre d'assistance utilisateurs handle requests pour emptying services.Le Help desk can either be a part of le municipality itself (Municipality -- Centre d'assistance) or can be a part of le private service prestataire (Service Provider -- Centre d'assistance).

E.  Guest: Guest utilisateurs have view-only access to le systeme et cannot perform any actions.

*Pre-Defined Roles in IMIS*

Le systeme inclut 17 pre-defined roles, categorized sous le respective utilisateur types. Chaque role has specific permissions tailored to its responsibilities.

A.  Municipality Roles

    1.  Municipality - Super Admin: Ce role is defined pour high-level ville officials avec full systeme access et management rights.

    2.  Municipality -- Executive: Ce role is defined pour high-level ville officials responsible pour overseeing municipal operations such as le Mayor, Deputy Mayor, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Administrative Officer,   Ward Chairmans, et so on.

    3.  Municipality - Building Permit Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversees le batiment permit processus, manages batiment permit approvals et lies services.

    4.  Municipality - Enquete batimentaireor: Ce role is defined pour le officials who collect le batiment footprint via le native mobile application (Building Information Collection Mobile App) of le IMIS.

    5.  Municipality - Infrastructure Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le utility et infrastructure management of le ville.

    6.  Municipality - Tax Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le property tax collection of le ville.

    7.  Municipality - Water Billing Unit: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le eau billing collection of le ville.

    8.  Municipality - Solid Waste Management Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le solid dechets management service fee collection of le ville.

    9.  Municipality - Sanitation Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le sanitation services being provided by le ville.

    10. Municipality - IT Admin: Ce role is defined pour le official ce is responsible pour systeme administration, utilisateur management, et technique appui et oversees IT lies issues et activities.

    11. Municipality - Public Health Department: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversees le public sante lies services et activities of le ville.

    12. Municipality - Centre d'assistance: Ce role is defined pour le officials ce oversee le help desk run by le municipality pour providing sanitation services.

B.  Service Provider Roles

    13. Service Provider -- Admin: Ce role is defined pour le person responsible pour le global administration et management of le  service prestataire.

    14. Service Provider - Vidange Operator: Ce role is defined pour  le staff of le service prestataire ce is deployed in le field  to provide emptying services, who use le Vidange Information Collection Mobile app    to collect emptying donnees.

    15. Service Provider - Centre d'assistance: Ce role is defined pour le staff of le service prestataire ce receives application requests pour  emptying service.

B.  Treatment Plant Role

    16. Treatment Plant -- Admin: Ce role is defined pour le staff of le traitement plant ce oversees le operation et management  of le traitement plant.

C.  Guest Role

    17. Guest: Ce role is defined pour such utilisateurs ce require view-only access to le systeme et its features, avec no permission to modify donnees.

Chaque role is assigned access to specific spatial layers et analysis outils based on le responsibilities, ensuring efficient systeme usage. Access to spatial layers et corresponding spatial analysis outils is granted to chaque role based on their responsibilities. Utilisateurs may have limited, or full permissions as obligatoire to perform their tasks efficiently. Municipality officials generally have broader access to spatial donnees, whereas Prestataires de services et Treatment Plant utilisateurs have role-specific access. Centre d'assistance utilisateurs have minimal access limited to processing requests, while Guests can only view publicly available spatial layers
